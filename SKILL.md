# Cross-Region IT Translation

## Reference Files
- [rules.md](./rules.md): Follow these translation rules and examples.
- [comparison_tables.md](./comparison_tables.md): Check regional equivalents for technical terms.

## Language Types:
- **English (Global)**: Clear, idiomatic technical English.
- **Simplified Chinese (Mainland China)**: Natural Simplified Chinese using Mainland terminology.
- **Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong)**:
    Traditional Chinese with a Hong Kong register.
    Depending on the audience, this can be formal written Chinese, written Cantonese,
    or a natural mix of Chinese and English technical terms.
